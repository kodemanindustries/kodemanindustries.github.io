# Swiftbits privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026.

Swiftbits is an emulator app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, made by Kodeman
Industries, LLC. It has no
accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer runs
no server that receives your data.

## What stays on your device

Your games, BIOS files, saves, save states, replays, settings and library live
on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, Swiftbits stores them in your own
iCloud account through Apple; the developer cannot see them.

## What leaves your device, and when

- **Cover art.** To show a game's cover, Swiftbits requests an image named
  after the game's title from the public libretro-thumbnails project on GitHub
  (raw.githubusercontent.com). The request includes the title and your device's
  network address, as any web request does. GitHub's privacy statement applies.
- **Game Guide (optional).** Nothing is sent until you enter your own Anthropic
  API key. Then each question you ask sends your question, a screenshot of the
  game, and a small amount of the game's memory to Anthropic's API under your
  key. The panel shows what was sent with each answer. Anthropic's privacy
  policy applies. Your key is kept in your device's Keychain and is not synced.
- **Game Center, Play Together, Net Play and SharePlay (optional).** Two-player
  sessions connect through Apple's Game Center or SharePlay to players you
  choose, and send the game's controller input and picture between your
  devices. The camera and microphone are used only for the optional video call
  in a session, when you turn it on. Apple's privacy policy applies.
- **Wi-Fi import (optional).** While the import screen is open, Swiftbits runs
  a small web page on your local network so you can send files from a computer.
  It closes when you leave the screen and is not reachable from the internet.
- **TestFlight.** If you test a beta, Apple shares crash reports and the
  feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight
  terms.

## Children

Swiftbits does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children.
